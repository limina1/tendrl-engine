#+TITLE: Annotations ledger — every note on a publication, on one surface
#+TYPE: feature
#+STATUS: building (Phase 5d; the table canvas it replaces shipped in 5b)
#+DATE: 2026-09-17
#+UPDATED: 2026-09-24

Plan Phase 5b built a *table*: fixed-size cards on a canvas, five
layouts, a spell header. Two weeks in the user called it "kind of clunky
for viewing the annotations, especially the table", and the redesign
that followed — a *ledger* — was approved with "this is a great design.
really conform to it." This note describes the ledger; the table's
canvas, its layouts and its pins are gone. Frozen design:
[[file:../annotations-ledger-contract.org][the Track 0 contract]]
(2026-09-24, replacing the table contract); approved prototype:
[[https://claude.ai/artifact/8ZTGgiDa6RPZGpRdWCWRkn][artifact 8ZTGgiDa6RPZGpRdWCWRkn]]
(version 5), committed as
[[file:../prototypes/annotations-ledger.html][prototypes/annotations-ledger.html]].
Six tracks build it in one worktree; the plan tracks them by checkbox
([[file:../insertions-plan.org][plan]] § Phase 5d).

* What it is

- *One buffer kind*, =annotations:<root>= (=30040:<pk>:<d>=), opened from
  the reader's Tools sheet (*table N*, N = notes, not requests) or
  =SPC b o t=. On desktop it *splits the pane* — reader | ledger
  (=store.splitFocused=); on the phone it is the focused work buffer,
  listed in the ☰ drawer. Beside the reader or filling the window is
  the WM's split state, not a mode of the ledger.
- *Rows, not a canvas.* One row per annotation: a 3 px rail in the
  author's colour, the =§= cell (click → the reader scrolls there), one
  glyph per kind — =◂= note (=▴= before · =▾= after) · =def= · =?=/=✓=
  request · =⑂= merge request · =⊞= kasten — the title, a "glossary"
  flag on a promoted definition, a "waiting" flag on an open request,
  "N ↳" for a request's answers, "↩ N" for its replies, the author, the
  age. A row opens *in place* (=Enter= or a click) with the full text,
  the per-kind actions and its thread; on a wide, unsplit ledger a
  *detail pane* on the right takes the body instead so the rows stay one
  line each (a legend toggle switches).
- *Four groupings*, one segment: /§/ (one group per TOC row in reading
  order, "whole publication" first; a request's answers *nest* under it
  with ↳), /author/ (most rows first), /kind/ (requests · merge
  requests · definitions · kasten · notes), /time/ (today · this week ·
  this month · earlier). =g= cycles them.
- *Two rows of chrome, every element a filter.* The spell input (the
  search DSL the engine mirrors: =A:<root> k:30041 [by:…] [a:<section>]
  [t:definition | t:annotation-request | k:818] [since:…] count:by
  count:a=; =Enter= re-parses a hand edit, a foreign root is refused, the
  wand saves it as a spell), *+ annotate*, the grouping segment,
  rows|board, then the facets: *who* (author bars with counts — the
  =count:by= buckets), *where* (=§n= chips with counts), *what* (kind
  counts), *when* (a 21-day sparkline, click a day; all · 7d · 30d). The
  timeline and collective layouts of the table survive here as the
  *when* sparkline and the who × where counts; the free layout and its
  pins were dropped.
- *Board* keeps a card view for reading content at a glance: one column
  per section, cards sized to their content, no absolute positions, no
  drag.
- *Colour is per author*, the highlight recipe (=discussions/colors.ts=,
  =pubkeyToColor=): the row rail, the dot, the bars, the thread rail —
  the same hue a person has on their highlights and comments.
- *The workflows hang off the rows.* =+ annotate= (or a section's =n=)
  opens the annotate window; an open request's row offers *answer it
  yourself ▸*; a vocabulary request keeps *define a term yourself ▸*
  after it is answered; a def row offers *relink* and *promote →
  glossary* (per event); the ⑂ row offers *review the diff ▸*; every row
  ends in its *thread*.
- *The reader folds its notes to a strip.* Under each section (and the
  whole-publication head) one stub per annotation — glyph · title ·
  "N↳" — and a click opens the full card under the strip, one open card
  per section. The 280 px margin column of the table epoch and its
  Reading preference are gone; the =card | sticky= skin stays. The
  section title row gains *n annotate*; a card gains *≡ row*, which
  opens the ledger if needed and reveals that row
  (=app.requestRevealAnnotation=).

* Answering by hand — one term at a time

The answer mode of the annotate window (the request card's or row's
*answer it yourself ▸*) takes the window's own shape and walks the
request's terms *one at a time*: the term is a dropdown listing the
request's terms with their state (" · defined by @assistant", " · signed
by you", " · skipped") and a last option *+ another term…* that swaps in
a free input; below it one definition entry, the format, the style, and
the event preview for that one 30041. *sign ▸* signs it (=role:
definition=, the =request= pointer, =sign_as: user=, local) and pages to
the next undone term; on the last one it closes. *skip ▸* pages without
signing. The header counts "i of n · k to do"; picking a term someone
already defined warns that this writes a second definition under your
key and that relink will ask which one to point at. An =ask= request
takes the same window with the question as a fixed title. A =notes= or
=kasten= request still hands off to the composer with the request
pointer. From vocabulary mode with words marked, *define them myself ▸*
walks the marked words the same way with no request at all — a
definition needs no ask.

* Responding to an annotation

Two paths, both on every row and every reader card, distinct by intent:
- A *reply* is a kind-1111 comment threaded under the annotation
  (NIP-22: =A= the annotation's coordinate, =K= 30041, =p= its author) —
  the row's thread shows the replies with the author rail, a reply box
  and the one-line tag preview; =Enter= signs. The annotation itself is
  never touched; the row wears "N ↩".
- A *note in reply* is a full 30041 anchored to the same section that
  carries a NIP-18 =q= tag naming the annotation it answers
  (=reply_to= on =POST /insertions=); the ledger shows "↩ <title>" on the
  row and the reader stacks them.
A request already had a third kind of response — the answer with the
=request= pointer — so the three are: a comment, a counter-note, an
answer.

* The review takes the reader's place

The merge-request review (=review:<id>=) stays a work buffer. Opened
from the ledger while it is split beside a reader, it replaces the
reader's leaf and *← reader* restores it. Top to bottom: the status
strip (badge · role · target · base against your current version), the
818's content with the requester's rail, *what changes* as tiles
(sections relinked · hunks · index changes · sections removed or moved ·
untouched), the definitions being linked as small cards (the reviewer
reads them; they become the hover text of every ref), one collapsible
diff per section, the index block, the assistant's review slot, the
thread on the 818, and a sticky footer — decline − · open merged result
in composer · merge + (armed). After the verdict the footer shows it in
place and the buffer stays open; the requester sees the same page
read-only.

* The boundary

Engine-side (=src/api/insertions.rs=): which 30041s anchor to the root,
their anchors, roles, request pointers and — new — =reply_to= (the =q=
coordinate) and, on request, the per-annotation kind-1111 count
(=replies: true= → one =#A= query over the loaded coordinates); the
=counts= histograms as before. =POST /insertions= takes =reply_to= and
emits the =q= tag; =propose_insertion= over MCP takes it too.

Web-side (=components/annotations/ledger-model.ts=, pure): =buildRows=
(notes + requests → rows), =groupRows= (the four groupings),
=flatOrder= (the cursor's order, a request's answers right after it),
=facetCounts= (each facet counted over the rows the /other/ facets
admit), =buildSpell= / =parseSpell=. Rows are a keyed ={#each}=; no d3.
The thread is one component (=AnnotationThread=) mounted by the row
body, the reader cards and the review.

Cross-buffer hooks (=state.svelte.ts=): =app.readerJump= and
=app.hoverAnchor= as before; =app.revealAnnotation= (root + id + nonce,
the reader card's "≡ row"); the page's =onAnnotationsTable= handler
opens or focuses the ledger. Live updates arrive through the store
stream ([[file:feat-insertions.org][insertions]] § Live) with
=concernsRoot=, debounced; a row that just landed wears =.new= once.

* Verification

=scripts/verify/annotations-ledger-seed.sh= +
=scripts/verify/annotations-ledger.mjs= — a CDP harness against a scratch
engine on =:3131= seeded with every row kind (notes by two keys, a notes
request answered, a vocabulary request answered by two definitions, an
open ask, a kasten bundle, two replies on a note, one open 818): the
split opens; groups per grouping; a facet filters and rewrites the
spell; a row opens with its thread and a reply signs; the ask is
answered by hand; a term is defined by hand on the answered vocabulary
request; a def row's relink opens the diff; the ⑂ row opens the review
in the reader's leaf and "← reader" restores it; the reader strip, a
stub's card, "≡ row"; hover lights the section; § scrolls the reader;
390×844 shows the phone rows. Recipe: =docs/commands.org= "Annotations
ledger — verify".
