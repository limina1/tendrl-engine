#+TITLE: Decision: Global Wikilinks Resolve to 30818 Only
#+TYPE: decision
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-08-12

* The decision

A global =[[wikilink]]= / ={{wiki:topic}}= resolves to *kind-30818 wiki
articles only*, matched by =d= tag (the topic *is* the article name,
NIP-54-normalized). The fallback that also admitted 30040/30041 by
their =T= title-slug is removed.

Document-local resolution is untouched: a topic naming a *sibling*
section of the containing publication (or draft) still resolves to it
first — that is an internal link, not a global lookup.

* Why

Each event class keeps its own referencing mechanism (user's call,
2026-08-12, during the on-device wiki-resolution pass):

- *30818 wiki articles* are communal topic pages — the natural, and now
  only, target of a global topic link.
- *30041 sections referencing each other* is what =ref:= and sibling
  resolution are for; zettel-to-zettel links live inside a publication's
  own structure. A global title-slug match made any same-titled section
  by anyone, anywhere, a link target — surprising, and wrong more often
  than right.
- *Open backlinks to arbitrary non-wiki events* are =embed:=/entity
  territory today, and a semantic-linking layer tomorrow — not something
  a title collision should approximate.

Bonus: the wiki lookup's relay traffic halves (one =#d= filter instead
of two), and the activity panel stops showing 30040/30041 fetches that
read as unexplained section pulls.

* What changed

=find_wiki_events= queries =k:30818 #d:<topics>= only; ranking is
author-preferred, then most recent (the kind-preference tier is moot).
Spec updated in =docs/nostrdown.org=; the regression test
=test_resolve_refs_wiki_30818_only= pins both halves (30818 resolves;
a T-slug 30041 no longer does).
