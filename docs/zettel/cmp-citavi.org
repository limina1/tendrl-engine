#+TITLE: Comparison: Citavi (Knowledge Organizer)
#+TYPE: comparison
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-07-05

The closest commercial ancestor of tendrl's composition model, and the
category's best *writing* workflow.

- *Knowledge items as first-class atomic objects.* Not notes-on-a-
  reference: direct quotations, paraphrases, summaries, image quotations,
  and source-less "thoughts" are individual records, each automatically
  carrying its source + page number. Reading produces pre-cited building
  blocks.
- *The category tree is the manuscript.* A hierarchical outline holds
  knowledge items (multi-assignment allowed, manually orderable);
  export produces a document ordered by the tree, every quotation
  dropping in with citation and bibliography intact. Writing becomes
  arranging evidence.
- *The inversion that matters:* the unit of writing is the idea/evidence
  *fragment*, not the source; sources become provenance metadata on
  fragments.

Mapping to tendrl: this is NKBIP-01, generalized — 30041 sections are
the knowledge items (with ={{quote:}}= carrying source attribution), a
30040 index is the category tree, and compose *is* the arranging. Where
tendrl goes further: Citavi's items live in a proprietary single-project
silo; ours are signed, globally addressable, reusable across
publications and authors, with fork lineage.

What Citavi has that we lack: the friction-free quotation capture flow
(select in source → knowledge item with locator, one gesture) — worth
holding as the bar for our highlight→section promotion
([[file:idea-annotation-anchoring.org][anchoring]], [[file:idea-see-also.org][promote gestures]]).

Cautionary notes: Windows-only, proprietary format, subscription drift
after acquisition — the exit-rights lesson from the other side.
