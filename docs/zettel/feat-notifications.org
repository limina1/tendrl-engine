#+TITLE: Notifications — what is for you, chosen by kind, never pushed
#+TYPE: feature
#+STATUS: plan (approved 2026-09-24; building as plan Phase 5c)
#+DATE: 2026-09-24

Not a social stream. tendrl lists *events of a kind that wants
something from you* — counted, never pushed — plus whatever activity
kinds you chose to hear about. Two surfaces, kept apart:

- *Choose* in the settings buffer (Settings › Notifications): a
  *checkable list* of the event kinds tendrl has a verb for, not a free
  kind-number field. A kind appears there the day the engine gains a
  verb for it. Each row: the kind, the engine's one-line rule ("9802 ·
  a highlight of one of your sections"), a checkbox, and whether it
  *counts* toward the modeline number or only *lists*.
- *See* on the 🔔 pill in the desktop modeline (next to agent
  services) and in the ☰ drawer's status section on the phone. Either
  opens the list: *asks* first (the fixed rows, which always show and
  need an act), then *activity* (the kinds you chose), then *mine* (the
  verdicts on merge requests you sent).

* The rule is the engine's, per kind

| Kind  | Addressed to you when…                            | Verb                      | Default |
|-------+---------------------------------------------------+---------------------------+---------|
| 818   | =p= you, or =a= one of your 30040/30041           | review, merge or decline  | on      |
| 7     | =e= an 818 you sent                               | see the verdict           | on      |
| 30041 =t: annotation-request= | =A= your publication, signed | answer or ignore        | on      |
| 1111  | =A=/=a= your coordinates, or =P=/=p= you          | read, reply in place      | off     |
| 9802  | =a= your coordinate, or =p= you                   | read                      | off     |
| 7     | =e=/=a= anything of yours that is not an 818      | read                      | off     |
| 9735  | =p= you                                           | read                      | off     |
| 30045 | =a= your 30040                                    | read                      | off     |
| any   | =p= you                                           | read                      | off     |

"Addressed to me" is a tag, not a search, so adding a kind is a
preference and the web never builds a filter. An unknown kind falls to
the generic =p= rule; a smarter rule is a code change on purpose. Muted
authors and the ignore list apply before anything is counted.
Persisted in =<data_dir>/notifications.json= — local state like
=relays.json=, never a Nostr event.

* Fetching

=GET /api/v1/notifications= reads the local store; the store stream
already announces every ingested kind, so an open list updates without
a refresh. Pulling from relays is a fetch like any other, Confirm-gated:
one filter per enabled rule (=#p= me, =#a=/=#A= on my coordinates) on
*my read relays* ∪ the provenance relays of my own events
([[file:decision-asks-routing.org][routing]]). A live subscription is a
follow-up and changes nothing here.

* Never a push

No OS notification, no badge that demands clearing. An ignored ask stays
open; the requester sees "open" and nothing more. This is the mission's
dial ([[file:mission.org][orientation dials]]) and the resurfacing idea's
"a modeline count, never a notification".
