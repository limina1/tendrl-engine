#+TITLE: Idea: resurfacing / deliberate review
#+TYPE: idea
#+STATUS: brainstorm — out of scope, captured for orientation
#+DATE: 2026-07-05

* What it means

The counterweight to a write-only archive. The universal failure of
highlight/note systems is that captured material is never seen again;
Readwise's Daily Review attacks it directly: a small scheduled dose
(5–15 items) of past highlights resurfaced on a spaced-repetition-ish
schedule, with lightweight feedback (keep / discard / favorite) and
user-tunable decay tiers. The point is not flashcard mastery — it is
that the knowledge layer stays /alive/.

* Data

Sources to sample from already exist as events: 9802 highlights,
={{quote:}}= quotes, standalone note 30041s, bookmarked items. Feedback
(discard/favorite/snooze tier) is local state, same store family as
[[file:idea-reading-states.org][reading states]] — publishing one's review
behavior is nobody's business (private layer later, gift-wrapped).

* Representation (engine)

- =GET /api/v1/review/sample?n=10= — weighted sampling over the knowledge
  layer: older + less-recently-resurfaced + favorited weigh up;
  discarded weighs to zero. A simple decay function first; real SRS
  scheduling only if the feature earns it.
- =POST /api/v1/review/feedback= — {id, action: keep|discard|favorite}.

* UI/UX

- A *review buffer* in the WM shell (research class): one item at a time,
  full provenance chrome, jump-to-source, and the promote gestures from
  [[file:idea-see-also.org][See Also]] (a resurfaced quote can become a ref
  in today's draft — review feeds writing).
- Entry point, not interruption: a modeline count ("12 to review"), never
  a notification (see [[file:mission.org][orientation dials]]).

* Open questions

- Sampling weights: age-based decay only, or engagement-informed?
- Does review include *sections you wrote* (self-review of drafts) or
  only captured material?
- Frequency cap / session length defaults.
