#+TITLE: Discussions & Highlights
#+TYPE: feature
#+STATUS: partial
#+DATE: 2026-07-05

Reading the conversation around a publication: NIP-22 comments (kind 1111)
and NIP-84 highlights (kind 9802) attached to sections and indexes. The
read side is shipped; the write side (authoring comments and highlights) is
planned — see =docs/discussions-plan.md=.

* What exists
- =POST /api/v1/discussions/counts= and =/discussions/list= (=src/api.rs=):
  per-address comment/highlight tallies, deduped engine-side, Confirm-mode
  gated like any fetch; counts ride along on list responses.
- NIP-22 threading runs engine-side (uppercase =A/E/K/P= root scope,
  lowercase =a/e/k/p= parent), per the frontend/backend boundary.
- NIP-84 highlight span resolution in =src/discussions.rs=
  (=resolve_highlight_spans=); the reader renders overlays via a
  =?highlight=<id>= buffer suffix (=web/src/lib/components/RichContent.svelte=,
  =HighlightList.svelte=, =HighlightsDrawer.svelte=).
- Per-section discussion badges in the reader (two-phase hydration), plus a
  "Refresh discussions" action.
- =DiscussionViewBuffer= (=web/src/lib/wm/renderers/DiscussionViewBuffer.svelte=):
  single-event view surfacing root/parent refs, loading sibling threads on
  demand; =CommentThread.svelte= renders trees.

* Gaps / open edges
- Write side unbuilt: posting comments/replies, capturing a selection as a
  highlight with =["offset", start, end]= anchoring, local-first authoring
  through the publish pipeline — all planned in =docs/discussions-plan.md=.
- NIP-25 reactions, notifications, and encrypted comments are explicitly out
  of scope for that pass.
