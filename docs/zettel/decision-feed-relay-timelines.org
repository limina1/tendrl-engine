#+TITLE: Decision: The Feed Is Per-Relay Timelines, Not One Composite Stream
#+TYPE: decision
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-09-10

* The decision

The main page lists *one relay's publications at a time*. Every relay the
local kind-30040s carry provenance from is its own timeline, picked from a
select in the feed header; "all relays" (the old composite) and "local
only" (signed here, never accepted) are the two non-relay entries. The
feed's second mode is the [[file:feat-bookshelf.org][bookshelf]].

* Why

- Provenance is the feed's organizing fact already (the relay pill on every
  row). A relay is a curatorial choice — thecitadel, theforest and
  mercury-relay hold different libraries — and a composite stream hides
  that choice behind a merge order nobody chose.
- It keeps the composite reachable (default "all relays") without making it
  the frame.

* Consequences / how

- Scoping is engine-side: =GET /api/v1/publications?relay=<url|local>= →
  =RelayTimeline= in =publication.rs=, applied after dedup and *before*
  the page limit, so a sparse relay still fills its page through the
  continuation loop. Under =fetch_always= the relay is also the sole
  fetch target ("Feed sync — <url>"). Rows for the picker:
  =GET /api/v1/publications/relays= (=stats::relay_provenance=, distinct
  coordinates per relay, not note versions).
- The selected timeline lives in AppState because every listing call
  carries it; feed responses discard themselves when the timeline changed
  mid-flight (the first composite load raced the first pick).
- Picker labels carry no counts: the engine tallies every index a relay
  holds, nested and empty ones included, while the feed lists roots.
- The provenance pill leads with the selected relay (display ordering).
