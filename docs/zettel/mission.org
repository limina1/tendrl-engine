#+TITLE: Mission
#+TYPE: meta
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-07-05

Tendrl is a local-first *reader, writer, and reference manager* built on
Nostr: read structured publications, research across a distributed event
network (structured search + semantic embeddings), compose and publish
NKBIP-01 publications, and manage references through the nostrdown
reference language.

Algorithmic, reader-driven fetching (post-hoc labels as retrieval signals)
is a *feature* of this mission, not part of it — see
[[file:idea-post-hoc-labeling.org][post-hoc labeling]].

* Emphasis tiers

Deliberately balanced, with a working order:

- *Top tier: writer + research.* Compose is the most mature surface;
  search/embeddings and the nostrdown layer are where the differentiating
  work is. Mechanics of knowledge management on Nostr come first.
- *Secondary tier: reader.* A reader is an aesthetic focus — important,
  but deferred. Reader-loop ideas are captured as brainstorm zettels
  (=idea-*.org=), not scheduled work.

* Orientation dials (not hard anti-goals)

None of these are absolute exclusions; they are dials whose setting follows
from the orientation above:

- *Social broadcast* — not the model; deliberate publishing is. Engagement
  metrics remain an option, never a default.
- *Relay browsing* — possible through relay provenance and deliberate
  searching; treated as a data-management concern, not a discovery feed.
- *Hosted service* — not a priority, but the architecture stays modular
  (swap the data store and multi-user hosting becomes possible). Fully
  valuable at n=1, offline, local copy is the archive.
- *Graph visualization* — knowledge *navigation* is the priority;
  visualization is an add-on if it proves useful (local neighborhoods
  only — see [[file:cmp-pkm.org][PKM comparison]] on the global-graph failure).
