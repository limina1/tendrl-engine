#+TITLE: Attached Events — the menu's publication row, chips, and the audio view
#+TYPE: feature
#+STATUS: implemented
#+DATE: 2026-11-09

Everything that hangs off a document is one hop away from its menu, and
audio is visible where the text is. Shipped on =feat/attached-events=.

* The gap it closes

The narration work ([[file:idea-audiobook-blossom.org][audiobooks on Blossom]])
made it plain: kind-32176 indexes and leaves were *hidden*. They were
attached to the publication index and its sections the same way
comments and highlights are — by coordinate — yet nothing in the event
menu said so, the profile had no column for them, and a 32176 found on
its own (search hit, profile, a relay) had nowhere to open.

* What exists

** The engine: =src/attached.rs=

=GET /api/v1/attached?coord=<kind:pubkey:d>= (or =?id=<event id>= for a
comment or highlight) answers with the *publication row* and five
buckets:

| bucket             | what                                                      |
|--------------------+-----------------------------------------------------------|
| =publication=      | the 30040 the target belongs to — itself for an index, the containing index for a section / recording, the root document for a comment or highlight; a standalone article / wiki is its own row |
| =comments=         | NIP-22 kind 1111 whose root or parent (=A= / =a=) is the target |
| =highlights=       | NIP-84 kind 9802 whose =a= is the target                    |
| =narration_index=  | kind-32176 roots (an =a=-children TOC) bound to the publication |
| =narration_leaves= | kind-32176 leaves (one =b= blob each) bound to the target — for a 30040, to any section it lists |
| =index=            | 30040s containing a section / narrated by a recording (empty when the target is one) |

=classify= is pure (bucket a bag of events against a =TargetShape=);
replaceables collapse to the newest version per coordinate. The handler
runs the local queries and the two fetch rounds of
[[file:decision-provenance-first-fetch.org][the provenance-first decision]]:
=policy=local_first= asks the relays the document came from, ungated;
=fetch_always= widens to the general set ∪ provenance ∪ hints behind the
usual intent.

Two narration lookups came with it (=src/narration.rs=):

- =GET /narration/node/:kind/:pubkey/:d= — recordings bound to one
  document on its own (article, wiki, spec, a section outside its
  publication), ranked against the local version; same two rounds.
- =GET /narration/event/:pubkey/:d= — a 32176 opened *as a book*: the
  event as a =Recording=, its chapters in =a= order (a leaf alone is one
  chapter), the version pin of each against the local node, what it
  narrates (=A= / =K=), whether that is local, and the local 30040 that
  lists a narrated section (=about_publication=).

** The event menu (=EventViewModal=)

Under the header, for every document kind (30040 / 30041 / 30023 /
30818 / 30817 / 32176 / 1111 / 9802):

1. the *publication row* — kind label, title; click opens that event's
   menu in place (breadcrumb back). Hidden when the event *is* the
   publication (the header already says so);
2. publisher · *published* date and time;
3. one chip per bucket, coloured: comments *orange*, highlights
   *yellow*, narration index *green*, 30040 index *blue*, audio leaves
   *purple*, each with its count. One event → the chip opens its menu;
   several → the chip unfolds a list of rows (author · label · date), a
   row opens the menu. A chip with nothing is dimmed, never hidden;
4. ⟳ runs the widening round; "via N relays" shows what the provenance
   round asked.

The first open per document in a session runs the provenance round;
later opens read the local store. A 32176's primary action reads
*Listen*.

** The per-chapter player strip (=NarrationStrip=)

Every content event with audio attached renders a slim strip at the top
of its body: ▶, narrator, voice, duration, progress while it plays, and
a *recordings* button that opens the drawer the way comments and
highlights open theirs. It replaces the ▶ that used to sit in the
section title. Inside a publication the strip plays the whole book from
that chapter; an article / wiki / spec on its own resumes from its
history entry (the document is the history key; a bare section is not
remembered). The doc view feeds it with one-node discovery
(=nar.refreshNode=), the reader with the publication's discovery.

** The audio view (=AudiobookBuffer=, =audiobook:<pk>:<d>=)

Opening a 32176 anywhere (search hit, profile tab, menu *Listen*) goes
through =app.openNarrationEvent=: when the narrated publication — or
the publication that lists the narrated section — is local, the
*reader* opens (the strips and ▶ listen are there); when it is not, the
recording opens like a book: title, narrator, voice, chapter count,
total length, *▶ play all*, and one card per chapter — ▶ from here,
duration, version pin, *open §* when that node is local, sources, the
transcript, ⋮ to its menu. ↻ fetches missing chapters (provenance
first, then the gated round). The listening place is remembered under
the narrated 30040 / document when there is one.

** Profile columns

*Narrations* (32176 roots) and *Audio* (32176 leaves) tabs, newest
version per d-tag, with voice, duration, section count and what they
narrate; a row opens through =openNarrationEvent=; *menu* opens the
event.

* Verified (2026-11-09, headless, isolated :3131)

Section menu → publication row + chips (narration index 2 · 30040 index
1 · audio 1); audio chip → the leaf's menu (breadcrumb); narration index
chip → list → a root's menu. Strip under each chapter; strip on a 30023
in the doc view, ▶ feeds the global player; its menu shows *audio 1*.
Profile Narrations (3) / Audio (3); a root whose publication is local →
reader; a root narrating a non-local publication → audio view with two
playable chapters. Provenance round in Confirm mode: a publication
fetched from theforest → =provenance= / =fetched_from= = theforest, no
intent, 0.6 s. Recipe: =docs/commands.org= "Attached events".

* Open

- Comments and highlights on *other sections* are not in a section's
  chips (per-event by design; the reader's discussion summary stays the
  publication-wide view).
- Nested 30040s: a 30040's "audio" chip covers its direct sections only.
- The audio view's *play all* is not device-tested on Android.
