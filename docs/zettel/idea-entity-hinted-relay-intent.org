#+TITLE: Idea: entity-hinted relays in the fetch intent
#+TYPE: idea
#+STATUS: open
#+DATE: 2026-07-06

A =naddr= / =nevent= is not just an address — its NIP-19 TLV carries *relay
hints*: "you'll find this event *here*." When a nostrdown =embed= / =quote= /
=slot= references such an entity and the event isn't local, the fetch should be
able to honour those hints, and the Confirm-mode intent should make them legible:
show *what* is being retrieved and *from where*, with the entity's own relay(s)
called out distinctly from the generic set.

* The gap today
- The entity fetch (=fill_from_entity= → =FetchAlways=) aims at the *search relay
  set*, ignoring the hints the entity itself carries.
- The [[file:feat-network.org][fetch intent]] shows that proposed set as a flat
  list — nothing says "this reference points *there*."

* The proposal
- *Engine:* decode the entity's TLV relays (=Decoded::Naddr.relays= /
  =Nevent.relays=, already parsed by =nip19=), aim the fetch at them, and put
  them on =FetchEvent::Intent= as a *distinct* field (e.g. =hinted_relays=)
  separate from the generic proposed set.
- *Web (=FetchConfirmModal=):* render the hinted relay(s) as a highlighted group
  labelled "this reference points here," with a quick *hinted-only / all-relays*
  toggle on top of the existing per-relay checkboxes.

* Why it's worth doing
Makes a relay fetch *legible and intentional* — you see the source and the
reason, and you choose the publisher's hint or the wider net. It's a small,
outbox-flavoured step (respect where the author said the event lives) without
committing to full NIP-65 routing.

* Feasibility — most of it already exists
- =FetchEvent::Intent= already carries a proposed =relays= list + a
  =RequestSummary=.
- =ConfirmDecision.relays= already lets the modal send back the *chosen* set,
  which =begin_operation= honours (=d.relays.unwrap_or(relays)=).
- =FetchConfirmModal= already does per-relay selection + add-relay.
Missing: the *hinted-vs-generic* distinction (engine field + fetch aim) and the
grouping/toggle (web). ~80% of the plumbing is in place.
