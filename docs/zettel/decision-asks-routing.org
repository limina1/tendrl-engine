#+TITLE: Decision: asks travel inbox-side — the owner's read relays and where the event was found
#+TYPE: decision
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-09-24

* The decision

Anything addressed to another pubkey — a signed annotation request on
their publication, a kind-818 merge request against their index or
section — goes to a *destination set* the engine builds from sources
it already has, each relay carrying its reason, and refuses rather
than guess when the two that matter are both empty:

1. *Their inbox*: the =read= entries of the owner's kind 10002
   (=RelayList::read_relays()=). Local store first; else resolved
   through the indexer composition the way =pull-user-data= does for
   your own 10002 — one Confirm-gated fetch, named as such.
2. *Where the annotated event was found*: nostrdb's per-note
   provenance (=note.relays()=, what the feed's relay badge reads) for
   the target section and its index.
3. *Relay hints* on the index's =a= tag for that section.
4. *Your write relays*, so the ask is findable from your side.

The Confirm modal lists every relay with its reason — /inbox/, /found
on/, /hint/, /yours/ — and the user may drop any. If 1 and 2 are both
empty the ask does *not* fall back to the general publish set
silently: the modal says so and offers the 10002 fetch. After
acceptance =record_event_relay= stamps the ask with its provenance.

The mirror holds for reading: =fetch= for notifications pulls =#p me=
(and =#a=/=#A= on my coordinates) on *my* read relays ∪ the provenance
relays of my own events — someone answers on the relay they found me
on, which may not be in my 10002 at all.

* Why

NIP-65: things addressed to you arrive on your read relays, where
others write to reach you; publishing to your own write relays would
put the ask where the owner never looks. Provenance is the second
source because a 10002 may be missing or stale, while the relay that
served the event to you is one the owner demonstrably published to.

* What exists, what is missing

Exists: 10002 parsing into read/write (=user_data.rs=), the own-10002
pull through the indexer composition, per-event provenance
(=record_event_relay= / =note.relays()=), and =union_relays= folding a
NIP-34 repository's relays into a broadcast set for issue comments.
Missing: =engine.inbox_relays_for(pubkey)= and
=destination_set(target_coord, owner) -> Vec<(url, reason)>=.
