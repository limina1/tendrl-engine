#+TITLE: Decision: merge requests on publications are NIP-54's kind 818, not NIP-34
#+TYPE: decision
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-09-24

* The decision

When someone wants an owner to take their change to a publication —
a relinked section, a glossary, a promoted insertion — they publish a
*derivative* under their own key and one *kind-818 merge request*
addressed to the owner. Kind 818 is NIP-54's: =a= the target, =e= the
base version, =e … source= the event to merge, =p= the owner; the
owner answers with a NIP-25 reaction (=+= merged, =-= declined, content
optional). The one extension: NIP-54 says the source MUST be a 30818;
here the target and source may be a 30040 or a 30041 of the same
kind, stated in [[file:../../nips/NKBIP-01-insertions.org][NKBIP-01
insertions]] § Merge requests.

*Merge* is the owner republishing their own version under their own
=d= from base + source, then reacting =+=. *Decline* is =-= with a
reason. "Partial" is not a third verb: the owner opens the computed
merged result in the composer, edits, signs, and the engine reacts =+=.
Status is derived on every read from the newest kind-7 by the target
owner (the =git.rs= pattern); nothing is recorded. Anyone may fork or
relink; the 818 is a request, not a claim. The derivative stays on
relays as the requester's fork.

* Why not NIP-34

- Its payload is git: a patch is =git format-patch= bytes, a PR a
  commit id on a clone URL. A publication has no repository; the
  version *is* the signed event, so NIP-34 would need a fake 30617 per
  publication or an invented diff format.
- Its status machinery assumes a maintainer set from a repo
  announcement; a publication's maintainer is the signer of the index,
  which the engine already knows.
- The fork half already exists: =fork_index= mints a derivative with
  =E=/=p= back-pointers. Only "please merge this" was missing, and 818
  is that event.

* Why 818 fits 30041s better than it fits wikis

NIP-54's =d= is the slug, so a fork is the same =d= under another key
and 818 exists to fold it back. A 30041 has an opaque =d=; two versions
by two people are two events, and an 818 between them says exactly
which one the owner is asked to take.

* Built (2026-09-24)

=src/publication/merge_request.rs= (818 template/parse, status from the
newest kind-7 by the owner, =fork_section=, =derivative_index=,
=destination_set=, =line_diff= / =index_diff=), =src/api/merge_requests.rs=
(=GET /merge-requests=, =POST /merge-requests/destinations=, =/react=,
=GET /:id/diff= — the owner's *next versions* computed against their current
version — =POST /:id/merge=, =/:id/decline=, =GET /:id/review=). A foreign
relink (=POST /publication/relink=) signs the derivative sections and index
and the 818 (source = index′). Web: the relink modal's fork path (the 818
preview, destination chips with reasons, the armed send) and the review
buffer (diff, event preview, decline − / merge + armed, verdict badges).

* Consequences

- The review is engine work: pair source with base (the 818's =e=, else
  the newest local version; say so when the target moved on), a content
  diff per section (line hunks, word marks), a structural diff of the
  index's =a= list, and — the point — *the owner's next version*, which
  is what the diff shows and what the armed button signs. Derivative
  sections therefore carry =E= to the copied version so they pair.
- Routing is inbox-side ([[file:decision-asks-routing.org][decision]]).
- An 818 for you is an *ask* ([[file:feat-notifications.org][notifications]]);
  it renders as an ordinary annotation card, ask colour, anchored to
  its target.
- The agent may review (=get_ask_diff= → =review_ask=, a local record)
  and prepare the merged draft; merge, decline, relink and the 818
  itself are always the user's key
  ([[file:decision-assistant-signs-own-key.org][decision]]).
