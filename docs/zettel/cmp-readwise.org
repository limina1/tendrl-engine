#+TITLE: Comparison: Readwise Reader (and the read-it-later class)
#+TYPE: comparison
#+STATUS: current
#+DATE: 2026-07-05

The state of the art in the *reader loop*. Load-bearing patterns:

- *Capture → inbox → triage.* Everything captured lands in an inbox; a
  fast keyboard-driven triage pass (Superhuman-inspired, deliberately
  game-like) moves items to Later or Archive; reading draws from Later.
  Archive ≠ delete. → [[file:idea-reading-states.org][reading states]]
- *Universal highlight layer.* One document+highlight schema across web,
  RSS, email newsletters, PDFs, EPUBs, YouTube transcripts, Kindle —
  heterogeneous sources normalized so downstream features are
  source-agnostic. Tendrl analog: everything becomes events; =document.rs=
  import is our normalizer.
- *Daily Review.* Spaced resurfacing of past highlights with tunable
  decay tiers — the fix for write-only highlight graveyards.
  → [[file:idea-resurfacing.org][resurfacing]]
- *Ghostreader*: LLM assistance in the reading flow (define, summarize,
  Q&A against the surrounding document) — no app switch. Tendrl's chat
  with context injection is the same move, local-first.
- *Export-as-integration*: continuous Markdown/JSON sync out to
  Obsidian/Notion; the pipeline, not captivity, is the product.

The category's structural warning: *Pocket* (Mozilla) shut down July
2025; unexported reading lists were permanently lost, and the export was
a flat CSV. Hosted read-it-later dies with its company. The durable
asset is the highlight/annotation corpus in an open format — which is
tendrl's native posture (local nostrdb + JSONL export + open protocol).
