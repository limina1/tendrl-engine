== What it is

The IAD framework operationalized for knowledge commons: biophysical
characteristics become ideas/artifacts/facilities, community becomes
users/providers/policymakers, rules-in-use at three levels; worked
example is a university digital repository. Ends by stating the
field's epistemic limit — the hinge this project's pitch turns on.

== The hinge quotes

Quote A — printed p. 60 / PDF p. 20, "Outcomes" section, verbatim:
"Most of the outcomes that have been written about in the newly
emerging knowledge-commons literature are either types of enclosures
of information that used to be open or the creation of new digital
commons that provide better access to information. Writers tend to
point to outcomes that they like or dislike, but few have gone into
in-depth analysis. Thus far we (all of us!) have mainly been at the
'look what is happening!' stage."

Quote B — printed p. 68 / PDF p. 28, chapter Conclusion, verbatim:
"Over time, it will be possible to extract design principles for
robust, long-enduring knowledge commons. After more efforts succeed
and others fail, we will be able to better understand what makes
various knowledge commons work and how we can better work toward
robust and sustainable resources."

"Being Prepared for Change" — named subsection, printed p. 68 / PDF
p. 28 (fifth adaptive-governance requirement, list introduced p. 66),
verbatim: "Institutions must be designed to allow for adaptation
because some current understanding is likely to be wrong, the
required scale of organization can shift, and biophysical and social
systems change. Fixed rules are likely to fail because they place too
much confidence in the current state of knowledge, while systems that
guard against the low-probability, high-consequence possibilities and
allow for change may be suboptimal in the short run but prove wiser
in the long run."

Together: Ostrom herself says analysis is stuck at spectatorship (A)
and understanding must wait for a success/failure record (B) — the
survivorship filter conceded in advance. Nostr converts waiting into
watching — link:claim-formation-observable.adoc[claim-formation-observable]; the change-preparedness
passage grounds link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract].

== Core claims

1. (41–42) IAD is a diagnostic tool for repeated rule-guided
   interaction; handles dynamic rule-formation, the hard and
   important case for knowledge. See link:concept-iad-framework.adoc[concept-iad-framework].
2. (42) Institutions = rules "understood and used by a community,"
   not what is written — link:concept-rules-in-use.adoc[concept-rules-in-use].
3. (46–48) Knowledge's biophysical layer = ideas/artifacts/facilities
   (Figure 3.2, p. 46); ideas "a pure public good and, therefore,
   nonrivalrous" (48) — link:concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities.adoc[concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities].
4. (49) Surprise of digital commons: "high degree of cooperation and
   coordination achieved by apparently disparate individuals" without
   face-to-face contact, despite homogeneity being the usual predictor.
5. (50) Rules analyzed at operational _ collective-choice _
   constitutional levels — link:concept-rule-levels.adoc[concept-rule-levels].
6. (51) Rules "hard to 'see,' as with protocols, standards, and
   computer code"; "technological inversion" when tech contradicts
   missions or rights; DMCA vs. fair use; DRM as private governance —
   link:concept-institutional-invisibility.adoc[concept-institutional-invisibility].
7. (51–52) Rule-making is often unselfconscious: teams adopt "a new
   rule... without ever self-consciously recognizing" the arena switch
   from operational to collective choice.
8. (52–53) Seven property rights for digital commons — adds
   contribution and removal to Schlager–Ostrom's five —
   link:concept-property-rights-bundles.adoc[concept-property-rights-bundles].
9. (55) Repository decision-making is "polycentric — ... decentralized,
   alternative areas of authority" — link:concept-polycentricity.adoc[concept-polycentricity].
10. (58) Perverse outcome is underuse; outside users are not free
    riders — "they enhance the quality of the resource by using it."
11. (61) Table 3.1 pairs outcomes: enclosure↔access, pollution↔quality
    control, noncompliance↔participation, withdrawal↔preservation.
12. (62–66) Six evaluative criteria: scientific knowledge growth,
    sustainability/preservation, participation, efficiency, fiscal
    equivalence, redistributional equity.
13. (66–68) Five adaptive-governance requirements (Dietz/Ostrom/Stern
    2003): information, conflict, compliance ("modest sanctions on
    first offenders," monitors "must be seen as effective and
    legitimate," 67), infrastructure, prepared-for-change —
    link:concept-adaptive-governance.adoc[concept-adaptive-governance], link:concept-graduated-sanctions.adoc[concept-graduated-sanctions].

== Hooks to the Nostr thesis

- Hinge quotes: the pitch's warrant (above).
- Unselfconscious rule-making (51–52) becomes loggable on Nostr:
  policy changes, NIP PRs, mute-list edits are published artifacts.
- Invisible rules (51) inverted: NIPs make the rule layer the visible,
  versioned layer — link:concept-governance-as-software.adoc[concept-governance-as-software].
- Legitimacy-of-sanctioners claim (67) testable per relay; exit via
  relay-list edits is the legitimacy meter — link:concept-exit-voice.adoc[concept-exit-voice],
  link:claim-split-sanctions.adoc[claim-split-sanctions], link:q-decoupled-vs-unitary-sanctions.adoc[q-decoupled-vs-unitary-sanctions].
- Being-prepared-for-change ↔ relay spectrum as institutional
  portfolio — link:claim-relay-spectrum.adoc[claim-relay-spectrum], link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract].

== Tensions / open questions

- Rules require "at least a minimal sanctioning ability" (50);
  kind-1984 reports decouple signal from sanction — strains the
  link:concept-adico-grammar.adoc[concept-adico-grammar].
- Exemplar arena is chartered and bounded; Nostr's constitutional
  level is protocol + rough consensus, no charter.
- Note 2 (68): left-side variables only "temporarily" exogenous, with
  feedback loops in fast systems — on Nostr the footnote is the main
  case (see link:agent-hess-ostrom.adoc[agent-hess-ostrom]).
