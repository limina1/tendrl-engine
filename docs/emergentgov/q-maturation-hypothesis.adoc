:type: question
:status: open
:tags: conjecture, maturation, long-horizon

A longer-horizon *conjecture the design is built to test* — always
marked as such, and kept distinct from the observable oscillation of
link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract] (standing
discipline: say which is which first).

The conjecture: as the stack matures, contraction depth shrinks and
re-expansion speeds up, until the loop effectively disappears — Nostr
as invisible protocol, the way HTTPS became invisible infrastructure.

What would count as evidence: a time series of shock episodes with
measurable contraction depth and recovery time; falsified if depth and
recovery don't trend, or if mature sub-networks still contract as hard
as young ones.
