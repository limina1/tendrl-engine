== The idea

All three tier-2 papers run Hirschman's exit/voice machinery through
blockchain, with forking as the crypto-native fusion of the two: exit
that takes the institution's code with you. Alston et al. make the
fork the constitutional escape valve — "the threat of a new competitor
is a strong governing force" (715) — while ordinary users, lacking
voice, "can only influence governance by exiting" (716). Allen et al.
recast forking from last resort to standing mechanism, the "calculus
of consent motivating exit" amplified by composability (663).
Murtazashvili et al. supply the synthesis line: Bitcoin's governance
"is disciplined by the fact that users can stay and participate or
can exit," and added exit options "create opportunities for voice"
(116). That last clause, and its shared load-bearing cite — Berg &
Berg 2020, "Exit, Voice, and Forking," common to all three papers —
is the citation bridge for the Nostr argument that exit REGENERATES
jurisdictions rather than merely leaving them.

== The Nostr engagement

Nostr is the limiting case that inverts the setup. In the blockchain
versions exit is costly (ledger network effects, 716) and forking is
rare and traumatic — two canonical war stories, block-size/BCH and
DAO/ETC. On Nostr keys and follow lists are portable, so exit cost
approaches zero; discipline operates continuously rather than
episodically, and "forking" degenerates into everyday relay choice or
relay founding — leaving can mean starting a jurisdiction
(link:claim-relay-spectrum.adoc[claim-relay-spectrum], link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract]). Voice also gains
what the blockchain papers lack: a typed, signed channel (kind-1984
reports, mutes) below the threshold of exit, with sanctions decoupled
from both (link:claim-split-sanctions.adoc[claim-split-sanctions]). Strain: if exit is nearly free
and continuous, does Hirschman's loyalty term vanish — and with it the
pressure that makes voice worth exercising at all?
(link:q-does-competition-improve-governance.adoc[q-does-competition-improve-governance]).
