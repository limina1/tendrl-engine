== Selection rationale

The four mapped chapters are the ones that analyze /governance
mechanisms of digitally-native commons/ (Schweik), supply the
libertarian/associational distinction the thesis is built on (Levine),
give a micro-institutional case of a bounded knowledge facility on an
open network (EconPort), and treat redundancy/persistence as an
institutional problem (Waters). The skipped chapters are advocacy,
legal theory, or stakeholder surveys whose thesis-relevant content is
duplicated more sharply elsewhere in the corpus.
