== What it is

The founding transfer of CPR theory to information, from the Duke
Public Domain conference (2001). Introduces the three-way
ideas/artifacts/facilities resource decomposition that the 2007 UKAC
framework chapter later imports as the "biophysical characteristics"
of knowledge (link:src-analyzing-knowledge-commons.adoc[src-analyzing-knowledge-commons]).

== Core claims

1. (112) Enclosure via law–technology conflict: "Information that used
   to be 'free' is now increasingly being privatized, monitored,
   encrypted, and restricted."
2. (113) "[C]ollective action and new institutional design play as
   large a part in the shaping of scholarly information as do legal
   restrictions and market forces."
3. (116–118) Hardin/Gordon/Scott theory is valid only as a special
   case (open access, no communication); organizing rules is itself a
   second-level dilemma, monitoring/sanctioning a third-level one.
4. (119–120) Goods classify on two axes, subtractability × cost of
   exclusion (Figure 1, p. 120); "common-property resource" conflates
   good with regime — see link:concept-cpr-vs-knowledge-commons.adoc[concept-cpr-vs-knowledge-commons].
5. (121) Facilities can be constructed CPRs: "mainframe computers and
   the Internet"; Internet flow units are "data packets or the
   computer files (information artifacts)" depending on layer studied.
6. (121–123) Open access ≠ common property; nationalizing de facto
   commons produced de facto open access — state control "less
   effective and efficient than control by those directly affected."
7. (124–127) Property = bundles of five rights (access, extraction,
   management, exclusion, alienation) held by positions from entrant
   to owner; "no set of property rights work equivalently in all
   types of settings" (127). See link:concept-property-rights-bundles.adoc[concept-property-rights-bundles].
8. (129–130) The namesake move: replace system/units with a three-way
   artifact/facility/ideas distinction (Figure 2, p. 129). See
   link:concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities.adoc[concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities].
9. (134) Licensing replaces sale → centralization → "less stable and
   more 'fugitive' artifacts, in that the publishers have the right
   of withdrawal." Lynch's Alexandria warning (137); LOCKSS as the
   decentralized-copies remedy (137).
10. (138) "[I]n the digital world, where no access is possible except
    by copying, complete control of copying would mean control of
    access as well" (quoting The Digital Dilemma).
11. (139–143) Counter-movement: arXiv (free, unrefereed, readers
    judge quality, 139); OAI interoperability standards where the
    artifact is a "record" (141); self-archiving authors participate
    "independently of governments and markets, in an international
    epistemic community" (143).
12. (143–144) Trusted Digital Repository attributes are explicitly
    called "design principles" for sustaining information as a CPR.
13. (144–145) "Governments, market forces, publishers, and traditional
    academic libraries can influence, but are not able to stop, the
    international movement of distributed information"; scholars shift
    from "passive appropriator" to "active provider."

== Hooks to the Nostr thesis

- Facility definition (129–130) reads as a relay spec: storage,
  membership rules, conduct rules, guards, maintenance.
- Right-of-withdrawal asymmetry (134, 144) inverted by multi-relay
  broadcast: LOCKSS-by-default — link:claim-no-global-bannability.adoc[claim-no-global-bannability].
- Flow rights vs. system rights held by different actors (126) =
  authorship decoupled from hosting.
- OAI records / low-barrier standards (141, 145) ≈ NIPs and typed
  event kinds — link:claim-composable-bounded-institutions.adoc[claim-composable-bounded-institutions].
- arXiv's reader-side quality judgment (139) prefigures decoupled,
  non-binding moderation signals — link:claim-split-sanctions.adoc[claim-split-sanctions].

== Tensions / open questions

- Threat model is enclosure; over-contribution (spam/pollution) and
  coordinated exclusion barely appear — link:q-goods-typology-fit.adoc[q-goods-typology-fit].
- Contributors assumed benign; no adversarial analysis — link:q-boundary-sybil.adoc[q-boundary-sybil].
- Removal rights treated as defect (fugitive archives, 144); Nostr
  makes artifacts effectively irrevocable, trading the fugitive
  problem for a right-to-be-forgotten strain.
