:title: Multiplicity — exclusion solved at every layer at once
:type: claim
:status: drafted
:date: 2026-08-04
:drafted-by: assistant
:tags: filtering, stack, redundancy, unenumerability

Exclusion is not solved once. The same function is solved at every
layer at once, by independent actors who never coordinate: internet
infrastructure (DNS, CDN) → relay filtering → named specs → client
web-of-trust the user picks. The space is open at both ends:

- *a-priori*: a developer guesses a threat in advance — e.g. a client
  auto-filtering anyone with 5 reports from your follow list.
- *a-posteriori*: a user adds quality after the fact — "I vouch for
  this person" attestations.

Standing discipline #1: name three or four methods as *samples from an
open space*, then refuse to finish the list — new ones ship faster
than anyone could catalogue them. **The unenumerability is the
finding, not a gap.** Enumerating "the N mechanisms" reintroduces the
taxonomy trap (contrast the closed 24-mechanism catalogue in
link:src-open-content-governance.adoc[src-open-content-governance]).
Ostrom's own 27+ boundary rules and "no analyst can perform a complete
analysis" (link:src-digging-deeper.adoc[src-digging-deeper],
pp. 90–91) is the direct ancestor of this discipline; requisite
variety is its theory
(link:concept-requisite-variety.adoc[concept-requisite-variety]).
