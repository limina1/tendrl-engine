== The idea

Goods classify on two independent axes: cost of exclusion ×
subtractability of use (V. & E. Ostrom 1977; Hess & Ostrom 2003,
pp. 119–120, Figure 1; UKAC Intro pp. 8–9, Figure 1.1). CPRs =
difficult exclusion + high subtractability. Three confusions to keep
apart (2003, pp. 118–123): the good (common-pool resource) vs. the
regime (common property) vs. no-regime (open access, res nullius);
and commons ≠ open access ≠ public domain (Intro pp. 13–14 — "a
commons is not value laden").

Knowledge inverts the CPR premise: ideas are nonsubtractive — "the
more people who share useful knowledge, the greater the common good"
(Intro p. 5) — so the classic appropriation dilemma weakens.
Knowledge-commons dilemmas are instead *pollution* (spam, no quality
control), *underuse/underprovision* (empty repositories,
anticommons — 2003 p. 128; UKAC ch. 3 p. 58, Table 3.1 p. 61), and
*enclosure*. The pivotal dynamic: "New technologies can enable the
capture of what were once free and open public goods," converting
them into CPRs needing management, monitoring, protection (Intro
p. 10). Sources: link:src-ideas-artifacts-facilities.adoc[src-ideas-artifacts-facilities],
link:src-ukac-introduction.adoc[src-ukac-introduction], link:src-analyzing-knowledge-commons.adoc[src-analyzing-knowledge-commons].

== Where Nostr sits awkwardly

Nostr straddles the typology's rows: events (artifacts) are
nonsubtractive and concurrently usable — public-good-like; but relay
storage, bandwidth, and human attention are subtractive — CPR-like.
So the network runs both dilemma families at once: pollution/spam is
appropriation of the subtractive layer through over-contribution to
the nonsubtractive layer. Exclusion cost is also layered: cheap per
relay (allowlists, filters), effectively infinite at the pool level
(no global bannability). The typology needs a per-layer application —
one classification per element of
link:concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities.adoc[concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities] — rather than one for "Nostr."
This is the core of link:q-goods-typology-fit.adoc[q-goods-typology-fit]; the capture dynamic (Intro
p. 10) is observable live as spam pressure converts open relays into
managed ones — link:claim-formation-observable.adoc[claim-formation-observable],
link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract].
