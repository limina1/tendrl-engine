== What it is

A glossary-style map of the entire Bloomington School vocabulary: IAD
core components, plus the adjacent frameworks the Ostrom chapter only
gestures at — polycentricity, ADICO grammar of institutions, property
rights bundles, local public economies, design principles, SES,
behavioral rational choice, and Vincent Ostrom's political theory.

== Core claims

1. p. 4 — "design" is a misleading D in IAD (presumes unbiased advice);
   development and diagnosis (finding "missing institutions") are apt.
2. pp. 5–6 — "governance determines who can do what to whom, and on
   whose authority"; can be "power with," not "power over."
3. pp. 6–7 — polycentricity: overlapping jurisdictions whose authorities
   "interact to determine the conditions" governing each other;
   multi-level/-type/-sectoral/-functional.
4. p. 7 — network-governance advocates err by "presuming that successful
   adaptations will emerge automatically from complex systems dynamics."
5. p. 7 — Ostrom 2010 recommends abandoning the arena/situation
   distinction; the action situation is the "black box."
6. p. 8 — action situations are nested and only "nearly decomposable";
   stability vs resilience vs robustness distinguished; robustness is
   the criterion for human-designed systems (p. 13).
7. p. 9 — level-shifting strategy: move an ill-served question to an
   adjacent arena; "also known as forum-shopping."
8. p. 10 — default conditions = institution-free baseline: "any player
   can retain any outcome that the player can physically obtain and
   defend."
9. pp. 11–12 — tragedy of the commons only in ungoverned open access;
   "drama of the commons" is the accurate phrase; a good's type varies
   by institutional setting.
10. pp. 13–14 — ADICO: strategies AIC, norms AIC+D, rules full ADICO;
    the Or Else names the actor "given the responsibility of imposing
    sanctions"; norms rest on socialization instead.
11. pp. 15–16 — property rights decompose (access, withdrawal,
    management, exclusion, alienation) into position-defining bundles;
    CPRs are not automatically common property.
12. pp. 17–19 — local public economies: provision ≠ production; "many
    units in a public economy monitor the activities of other units";
    coordination without a single center; citizen choice/voice/exit.
13. p. 19 — design principles "perhaps better described as good
    practices": observed correlates of long-term sustainability.
14. p. 22 — cooperation factors include known reputations, "capacity to
    enter or exit from a group," communication, sanctioning capability.

== Hooks to the Nostr thesis

- ADICO with no Or Else: a kind-1984 report is norm-shaped; enforcement
  is crowdsourced — link:claim-split-sanctions.adoc[claim-split-sanctions], link:concept-adico-grammar.adoc[concept-adico-grammar].
- Default conditions ≈ the open relay pool; gated relays are boundary
  overlays that never delete the defaults — link:claim-composable-bounded-institutions.adoc[claim-composable-bounded-institutions].
- Citizen exit + forum-shopping = relay-hopping — link:concept-exit-voice.adoc[concept-exit-voice],
  link:concept-interjurisdictional-competition.adoc[concept-interjurisdictional-competition].
- Distributed specialist monitoring (p. 18) = label providers, WoT
  scorers, report bots — link:claim-multiplicity-stack.adoc[claim-multiplicity-stack].
- Microsituational cooperation variables (p. 22) are measurable in the
  wild on Nostr — link:claim-formation-observable.adoc[claim-formation-observable].

== Tensions / open questions

- Polycentricity requires inter-authority interaction; relay operators
  mostly don't interact — protocol substitutes for negotiation
  (link:concept-polycentricity.adoc[concept-polycentricity], link:concept-governance-as-software.adoc[concept-governance-as-software]).
- Norms enforced only where widely shared (p. 14); Nostr enforcement
  rides operators' private incentives instead — link:q-decoupled-vs-unitary-sanctions.adoc[q-decoupled-vs-unitary-sanctions].
- Design principle 7 (recognition by higher authorities) has no referent
  on Nostr; principle 1 and 5 are the ones Nostr disassembles.
- Robustness of what unit — relay, relay set, or protocol? —
  link:q-generativity-robustness.adoc[q-generativity-robustness], link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract].
