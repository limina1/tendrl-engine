== The idea

Ostrom 1990's eight design principles, as enumerated by McGinnis
(pp. 19–20): (1) clearly defined boundaries (biophysical and social);
(2) congruence between appropriation/provision rules and local
conditions; (3) collective-choice participation by most affected;
(4) monitors accountable to (or drawn from) appropriators;
(5) graduated sanctions; (6) low-cost dispute resolution; (7) minimal
recognition by higher authorities of rights to self-organize;
(8) nested enterprises. McGinnis hedges the epistemics himself: they
are "perhaps better described as good practices" — "characteristics...
that have been observed to be regularly associated with the long-term
sustainability" of CPR systems, not all needed in all circumstances
(p. 19). Ostrom 2007 describes them as distilled from "robust,
self-organized institutions for achieving sustainable resource use of
very long periods of time" (p. 48) — i.e., read off institutions that
had already survived. Cox et al. later disaggregate several (McGinnis
p. 20).

== The Nostr engagement

The survivorship framing is the thesis's foil: the principles are
correlates recovered from winners, and the method cannot watch
formation — see link:concept-survivorship-bias.adoc[concept-survivorship-bias] and
link:claim-formation-observable.adoc[claim-formation-observable]. Against the list: principles 1 and 5 are
the two Nostr visibly disassembles (elastic per-relay boundaries,
link:concept-boundary-rules.adoc[concept-boundary-rules]; decoupled emergent sanctions,
link:concept-graduated-sanctions.adoc[concept-graduated-sanctions]). Principle 7 is satisfied vacuously —
there is no higher authority to grant recognition; censorship
resistance substitutes. Principle 8 (nested enterprises) is arguably
what bounded institutions composing on the open substrate look like —
link:claim-composable-bounded-institutions.adoc[claim-composable-bounded-institutions]. Whether principles are
predictive for relays observed mid-formation is a testable question —
link:q-maturation-hypothesis.adoc[q-maturation-hypothesis].
