== The idea

For natural-resource CPRs the working distinction is resource system
vs. resource units (fishery vs. fish). Hess & Ostrom argue that for
knowledge this "two-way distinction is not as useful as a three-way
distinction": *facilities* store artifacts and make them available (a
resource system with membership rules, conduct rules, guards,
maintenance costs); *artifacts* are "discreet, observable, nameable
representation[s] of an idea" — the physical flow units of a
facility, sequential-use when physical, concurrent-use when digital;
*ideas* are the nonphysical flow units contained in artifacts, pure
public goods, nonrivalrous. Sources: Hess & Ostrom 2003, pp. 128–134
(Figure 2, p. 129) — link:src-ideas-artifacts-facilities.adoc[src-ideas-artifacts-facilities]; imported as
the IAD's biophysical layer in UKAC ch. 3, pp. 46–48 (Figure 3.2) —
link:src-analyzing-knowledge-commons.adoc[src-analyzing-knowledge-commons].

The 2003 article adds a dynamic corollary: when artifacts are
licensed rather than sold, they centralize and become "fugitive" —
"the publishers have the right of withdrawal" (2003, 134); Lynch's
Alexandria warning (137) and self-archiving's submit/unsubmit problem
(144) are both withdrawal-rights pathologies. The era's remedy was
LOCKSS: deliberate decentralized copying (137).

== The Nostr mapping

Facilities ↔ relays (the 129–130 facility definition is a relay spec:
storage, availability, legitimate-user rules, conduct rules, guards).
Artifacts ↔ signed events — with cryptographic nameability (event
ids, signatures, typed kinds) exceeding anything the schema imagined;
OAI's "record" (2003, 141) is the nearest ancestor. Ideas ↔ the
discourse layer: threads, memes, the medical knowledge carried by
bounded communities on the substrate.

Nostr inverts the withdrawal asymmetry: broadcast to N facilities
means no actor holds withdrawal rights over the pool —
LOCKSS-by-default, and the mechanism behind
link:claim-no-global-bannability.adoc[claim-no-global-bannability]. Deletion (kind-5) is advisory, so the
"fugitive artifact" problem disappears and its mirror image appears:
effectively irrevocable artifacts and a right-to-be-forgotten strain.
The removal right the 2007 chapter added for knowledge commons
becomes per-facility and unenforceable at the pool level — see
link:concept-property-rights-bundles.adoc[concept-property-rights-bundles].
