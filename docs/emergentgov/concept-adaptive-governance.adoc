== The idea

The cybernetic reading of governance: a system acts on information
input and feeds its own action back into subsequent behavior. The
homeostatic model has three elements — circular causality, tendency
toward stability, and information flows linking them (Lewis & Aligica
2024, p. 8). Negative feedback is error-correcting; positive feedback
is runaway. Ashby's challenge (the autopilot, pp. 9–10): no
first-order machine can switch itself from error-aggravating to
error-mitigating feedback; a meta-level variable alpha is needed —
"the part can be self-organizing within the whole S + α" (p. 9).
Self-governance therefore requires compounded hierarchies of feedback
mechanisms — second-order cybernetics, strange loops (p. 10).

In IAD terms the alpha is the higher rule level: dissatisfied actors
level-shift, using collective-choice rules to change operational rules
— rule change *is* the negative feedback (pp. 12–13). This is why the
three levels of rules are "an expression of the underlying logic of
the very phenomenon of self-organization" (p. 10), and why deep-level
change being harder and costlier (Ostrom 2005/2025, p. 96) stabilizes
the loop rather than obstructing it.

== The Nostr case

Expand–contract boundary dynamics are homeostasis in the wild: a spam
wave hits, boundary rules tighten, pressure passes, they relax — and
every correction is a signed, timestamped event, so the loop's wiring
is observable rather than inferred (link:claim-expand-contract.adoc[claim-expand-contract],
link:claim-formation-observable.adoc[claim-formation-observable]). The strain: Ashby's alpha is nested
*above* its loop, but Nostr's exclusion layers (client mute, relay
policy, WoT score, curated list) are parallel and uncoordinated —
each acts as alpha for failures of the others without standing over
them. The Nostr case suggests a lateral, plural alpha: redundant
peer-level correctors substituting for a meta-level, an extension
second-order cybernetics does not contemplate. And with no shared
setpoint across relays, what is held homeostatic is the substrate
(protocol interoperability), not community outcomes — distinguish
substrate-homeostasis from community-homeostasis.
