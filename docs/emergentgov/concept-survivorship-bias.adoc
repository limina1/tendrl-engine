== The idea

The design principles were harvested from the survivors. The founders
say so themselves: the eight principles "were discovered after
conducting a large set of empirical studies" comparing already-robust
with already-failed systems; "they are in no way prescriptive—nor are
they models"; they come from "small, homogeneous systems," and
whether they apply to knowledge commons "is a question for further
research" (UKAC Intro p. 7 — link:src-ukac-introduction.adoc[src-ukac-introduction]). The method is
retrospective by construction: you identify what robust institutions
have in common only after robustness and failure have sorted the
sample. Formation — the rule-by-rule assembly of an institution — was
never in the data.

For knowledge commons the founders concede the limit twice more, in
the hinge quotes (UKAC ch. 3, p. 60 "look what is happening!" stage;
p. 68 "After more efforts succeed and others fail, we will be able to
better understand what makes various knowledge commons work" — full
verbatim text and PDF locators in
link:src-analyzing-knowledge-commons.adoc[src-analyzing-knowledge-commons]). Analysis in 2007 was spectatorship
plus a promissory note: wait for a mortality record, then induce.

== Why Nostr breaks the limit

This concept is the theoretical backbone of
link:claim-formation-observable.adoc[claim-formation-observable]. On Nostr the formation process emits its
own archive: every report (kind-1984), mute list, follow, relay
policy change, and NIP is a signed, timestamped, typed event. The
success/failure record Ostrom said we must wait for accrues in public
and in real time, including the failures — dead relays, abandoned
NIPs, collapsed communities — which retrospective sampling
systematically loses. The maturation question is whether principles
induced from formation-stage data converge on the retrospective
eight or amend them — link:q-maturation-hypothesis.adoc[q-maturation-hypothesis],
link:concept-design-principles.adoc[concept-design-principles]. Caveat to carry: observability of
formation does not guarantee representativeness — Nostr is one
substrate, and its own survivors will be overdocumented relative to
its failures unless failure events are archived deliberately.
