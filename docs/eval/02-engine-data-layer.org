* Findings (prioritized)

** [High] Set-name → field routing duplicated 4× across two files
   - where :: [[src:src/engine.rs::892][engine.rs:892]], [[src:src/engine.rs::944][:944]], [[src:src/relay_store.rs::241][relay_store.rs:241]], [[src:src/relay_store.rs::375][:375]]
   - what :: The flat+dotted set-name match
     (=general=/=publish=/=fetch=/=broadcast=/=search.default=/=search.fallback=/
     =indexer.default=/=indexer.fallback=) is hand-rewritten in =Engine::add_relay=,
     =Engine::remove_relay=, =RelaySets::get=, =RelaySets::get_mut=, plus the
     sibling-strip table in both =Engine::add_relay= and =RelayStore::add=. Two layers
     (=Engine= over in-memory =RelayConfig=, =RelayStore= over =RelaySets=) maintain
     parallel copies of the same routing logic and the same per-URL mutual-exclusion
     rule; =relay_sets_snapshot_locked= manually mirrors 8 fields at [[src:src/engine.rs::694][engine.rs:694]].
   - why :: Adding a 6th relay class means editing ~6 match arms in 2 files; they drift.
   - action :: Make =RelaySets= the single source of truth and have =Engine= re-derive
     =RelayConfig= URL fields, or push =add=/=remove= entirely into =RelayStore=.

* Roadmap alignment

The relay-class machinery (broadcast/search/indexer with default/fallback tiers,
exclusive flags, =CompositionShape=/=PhaseStage= composition, backward-compat flat-array
deser) is *coherently implemented, not half-migrated* — =relay_store.rs= and
=compose_discovery_phases= / =fetch_with_composition= ([[src:src/engine.rs::403][engine.rs:403]]) line up with the
documented matrix, with tests covering tier migration and exclusive round-trips.
FetchPolicy and the Confirm-mode gate are consistently threaded through every fetch
path. The one incompleteness is =broadcast= (a field + accessor [[src:src/engine.rs::297][broadcast_relays() :297]],
persisted, but no routing yet) and =slot_in_version=/forking being half-wired. (See
[[file:04-api-network-identity.org][04]] theme 3 for the API-side view of this.)

* Themes
1. *Relay-set routing is the worst duplication* — collapse to one source of truth.
2. *Event construction is copy-pasted* — unify the 4 flat/block builders on
   =tree_emit::sign_event=.
3. *Confirmed dead code* — relay.rs helpers + 2 PublicationEngine methods; safe to delete.
4. *Two 3100-line files mix too many concerns* — split relay-mgmt + embedding-sync out
   of engine.rs; event-building out of publication.rs.
5. *Patterns are otherwise consistent* — FetchPolicy/Confirm/LoadStatus/read-lock are
   uniform; only divergence is relay_store.rs's separate error type (silent saves).
