** The governing principle (memorize this)
- *Rust* owns fetching + events + all /algorithmic derivation of structured data/
  (parse, assemble/order tree, decode/encode NIP-19/11, dedup, slug gen, threading,
  highlight resolution).
- *TypeScript* owns rendering + /ephemeral view/interaction state/ + calling Rust.
- When the same logic exists in both, resolve *toward Rust* (build/wire Rust, delete
  the TS twin) — never the reverse.

** The two directions of "shedding" (don't conflate them)
- The *Rust TUI state-machine* is shed FROM RUST (delete) — but keep the pure core.
- The *NIP transforms* are missing from Rust; you BUILD them in Rust and shed the
  *TypeScript* copies. You are not deleting a capability.
