== Governing the Decentralized Commons

{{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:governing-the-decentralized-commons|Governing the Decentralized Commons}} — the author's earlier paper (the preface calls
it "largely written by AI for the purpose of a research program" and
released with "personal refinements"): "the first application of
institutional analysis to a censorship-resistant social protocol."
Maps IAD components to Nostr observables, and reads Ostrom's fifth
design principle into a five-level graduated-sanctions hierarchy —
follow lists (kind 3) as implicit endorsement, mutes, reports (kind
1984), relay policy, network exclusion. Notes the interplay of rule
levels: users dissatisfied with relay policy "can establish their
own relays, essentially exercising constitutional rights to
self-organize and exit." The emergent-governance kasten is this
paper's successor, with the unitary-sanctions assumption dropped
(link:q-quality-without-gatekeeping.adoc[quality]).

== Understanding Knowledge

{{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:understanding-knowledge|Understanding Knowledge}} — the Alexandria essay. Its material cause is the
*semantically closed unit* (a note that carries its own meaning) and
Herbert Simon's near-decomposability; its formal cause is the
*rhizome* after Deleuze and Guattari — non-hierarchy, multiplicity,
asignifying rupture, cartography ({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:ii-formal-cause-the-structure-and-organization-of-knowledge|II. Formal Cause}}); its final
cause names NKBIP-01 and NKBIP-02 as the affordances for a
rhizome-shaped knowledge base ({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:iv-final-cause-the-purpose-of-knowledge-and-knowledge-ecosystems|IV. Final Cause}}). The essay's
closing note — "You don't have ideas, ideas have you. They enter your
head through interactions and observations of the world" — is the
seed of the anchor section's first sentence, "Ideas arrive like
uninvited guests." Hess and Ostrom's *artifact* is the semantically
closed unit given a signature.

== Thinking Through Emergence: A Framework Bridge

{{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:thinking-through-emergence-a-framework-bridge|Thinking Through Emergence}} — the source of the essay's closing vocabulary,
"the endless territorialization and deterritorialization of
understanding" (Part II, {{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:thinking-through-emergence-a-framework-bridge-part-ii-territories-and-deterritorializations|Part II: Territories and Deterritorializations}}), and of its
process ontology ("Everything flows"). It also contains the James C.
Scott case study that the scenius transcript below leans on. The
emergent-governance kasten's formation episode is a
deterritorialization–reterritorialization cycle written in
institutional terms
(link:claim-enclosure-release-is-expand-contract.adoc[expand–contract]).

== From 99% Junk to Scenius at Scale (DWeb 2026 transcript)

{{ref:30818:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:from-99-junk-to-scenius-at-scale-why-the-future-of-work-is-an-economy-of-play-dweb-2026|From 99% Junk to Scenius at Scale}} — a kind-30818 wiki page transcribing a talk
on major evolutionary transitions and the economy as a collective
intelligence. Its central complaint is legibility: large coordination
systems "coarse grain the information they're gathering," so "most
of what people do in industrial and modern economies is illegible to
those economies," and funding "overindexes on legible signals" until
attention gathers "in very narrow regions of the space of all
possible ideas." That is the essay's "institutional capture" as a
scaling pathology, and its remedy — scenius, a scene's communal
intelligence — is the essay's "ecology of conversation"
(link:concept-facilitated-promiscuity.adoc[facilitated promiscuity]).
The transcript's Santa Fe Institute references connect it to the
essay's complexity-theory example.
