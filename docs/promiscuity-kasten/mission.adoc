This kasten is an answer to one annotation request, signed on the
section *The Natural Promiscuity of Understanding*
({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:aktaotwp-the-natural-promiscuity-of-understanding|The Natural Promiscuity of Understanding}}) of the essay _Anarchistic Knowledge: The
Art of Thinking Without Permission_ ({{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:anarchistic-knowledge-the-art-of-thinking-without-permission|Anarchistic Knowledge}}). The request
asked for research that ties the essay to Ostrom's work on governing
digital and knowledge commons and to the author's own kasten on
emergent governance on Nostr ({{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:emergentgov|emergent governance on Nostr}}), and to "tile up this
work with relevant information, links and ideas."

The tiling has one thesis. The essay describes the *idea layer* of a
knowledge commons — the layer that Hess and Ostrom call nonrivalrous
and "enriched rather than depleted by use." Ostrom's institutional
work describes the *facility layer* — the departments, journals,
relays and communities that store ideas, admit people, and sanction
conduct. The essay's "anarchistic knowledge" is not knowledge without
institutions; it is knowledge whose institutions are plural,
composable, and boundary-elastic, sitting on an open pool. That is
exactly the shape the emergent-governance kasten finds on Nostr, so
the two bodies of work are one argument seen from two layers.

Each note here takes one move in the essay, names the Ostrom or
emergent-governance idea it instantiates, cites the source, and says
where the fit strains. The link:map-tiling.adoc[tiling map] lays the
notes over the essay section by section. Everything below the meta
layer is `status: drafted` — machine-written and unverified until the
author reads it against the sources.
