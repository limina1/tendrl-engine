== What it is

The author's own kasten ({{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:emergentgov|emergent governance on Nostr}}): a framework for
*emergent governance itself* — institutional formation as an
observable process — with Nostr as the demonstration case. Its
narrative ({{ref:30040:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:emergentgov-the-narrative|The narrative}}) runs: the question (how is there
quality discourse on the most adversarial substrate that exists);
the founders' limit and its inversion (Hess and Ostrom's "look what
is happening!" becomes method when what is happening arrives as
signed events); the object (a formation episode: shock → response
portfolio → adaptation → settlement or failure); a spectrum instead
of a wall; multiplicity; sanctions come apart; how it holds over
time; how we know. Notes are cited here by their published
coordinate stems.
