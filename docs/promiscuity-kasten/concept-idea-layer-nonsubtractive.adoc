== The idea

Hess and Ostrom split a knowledge commons into three resource types:
*facilities* store artifacts and make them available (with membership
rules, conduct rules, and maintenance costs); *artifacts* are
"discreet, observable, nameable representation[s] of an idea"; and
*ideas* are the nonphysical flow units contained in artifacts, pure
public goods, nonrivalrous ("Ideas, Artifacts, and Facilities,"
_Law and Contemporary Problems_ 66, 2003, pp. 128–134; carried into
_Understanding Knowledge as a Commons_, 2007, ch. 3). The premise the
whole knowledge-commons school rests on: "the more people who share
useful knowledge, the greater the common good."

The essay's opening section is a description of that idea layer from
the inside. "When knowledge flows freely, it doesn't respect the
borders we've drawn around subjects" — that is nonsubtractability as
lived experience. Biology "bleeding" into architecture costs biology
nothing. The essay's word for it, *promiscuity*, is a stronger claim
than nonrivalry: ideas are not merely undiminished by sharing, they
actively seek unauthorized partners.

== Where it strains

The knowledge-commons literature replaces the appropriation dilemma
with two others — *pollution* (spam, no quality control) and
*underprovision* — and one dynamic, enclosure: "New technologies can
enable the capture of what were once free and open public goods"
(Hess & Ostrom 2007, intro p. 10). The emergent-governance kasten
notes that Nostr runs both dilemma families at once because events
are nonsubtractive while relay storage and human attention are
subtractive ({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:concept-cpr-vs-knowledge-commons|cpr vs knowledge commons}}). Read back
onto the essay: promiscuity is free at the idea layer and *costly at
the facility layer*. A mind that follows every tangent pays in
attention; a department that admits every cross-pollination pays in
coherence. The essay's later admission that "most branches wither"
and "noise often drowns signal" is the pollution dilemma arriving
where the opening section had only celebrated flow.

The three-way decomposition also gives the essay's Nostr paragraph
its precise form: relays are facilities, signed events are artifacts
with cryptographic nameability, and the discourse is the idea layer
({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:concept-ideas-artifacts-facilities|ideas artifacts facilities}}). The essay's hope
that Nostr can host commons "that can't be enclosed or controlled by
any single entity" is Hess and Ostrom's withdrawal-rights argument
inverted: broadcast to many facilities means no actor holds
withdrawal rights over the pool.
