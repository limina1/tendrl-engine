== The idea

Ostrom's first design principle is the clearly defined boundary, and
her rule typology names the mechanism: *boundary rules* "specify how
participants enter or leave positions" — residency, credential,
permit, fee, lottery (McGinnis's IAD guide, p. 10; Ostrom 2007,
pp. 38–39; {{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:concept-boundary-rules|boundary rules}}). The essay's
"institutional boundaries that separate fields" are boundary rules
for a knowledge facility: the department admits by credential, the
journal by review, the tenure track by conformity to "established
patterns of thought." The essay does not deny that these boundaries
do work — "certain insights only emerge under specific pressures" —
it objects that they also "block the natural tendency of ideas to
cross-fertilize."

The emergent-governance kasten's finding is that on Nostr boundary
rules "do not disappear; they multiply, localize, and become
elastic." Relays sit on a spectrum with three poles: the hard
allowlist floor, the open pool, and in the middle the curated relay
that "still speak[s] the base event kinds, so a user joins a real
community without leaving the commons"
({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:claim-relay-spectrum|relay spectrum}};
{{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:claim-composable-bounded-institutions|composable bounded institutions}}).

== The mapping

Lay the spectrum over the essay's landscape of knowledge spaces:

- *Allowlist floor* ↔ the department, the credentialed journal: a
  bounded institution, membership set by rule, high-pressure, high
  coherence. The essay's "pressure chamber."
- *Open pool* ↔ the essay's "underground networks of understanding":
  the mechanic, the bartender, the gardener — thinking under what the
  IAD tradition calls default conditions, no entry rule at all.
- *Curated middle* ↔ "interdisciplinary ventures," hackerspaces, the
  online forum that "puzzle[s] through problems together": bounded
  enough to sustain discourse, still speaking the common language.

The essay's own answer — knowledge "forming into departments then
dissolving into interdisciplinary ventures" — is the spectrum
traversed in time. What Nostr adds, and what the essay's Nostr
paragraph gestures at without the vocabulary, is that the poles
*compose*: a person can be inside the allowlisted community and the
open pool at once, with one identity, because keys and follow graphs
leave with the user ({{ref:30041:dc4cd086cd7ce5b1832adf4fdd1211289880d2c7e295bcb0e684c01acee77c06:concept-exit-voice|exit voice}}). Academia has
no equivalent: exit from a department is exit from its facility.
